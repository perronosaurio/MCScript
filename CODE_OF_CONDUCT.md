# Code of Conduct

MCScript is a small project made by people who like block games. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their
age, background or experience, and we want it to stay a friendly place to ask questions and share work.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to programming or to ClassiCube.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Explain why when you turn something down.
- Assume good intent, and take it well when someone points out a mistake.
- Keep discussions on topic and in English, so everyone can follow them.

## What isn't accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or slurs of any kind.
- Sexual content, or attention someone has asked you to stop.
- Posting someone's private information (real name, address, IP address) without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, or trolling.
- Posting exploits for other people's servers, or asking for help to grief or attack them.

These rules cover everything in this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits) and any other
space run in the name of MCScript.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, use **Report content** on the comment or issue (the `...` menu) and choose to
report it to the repository maintainers, or contact the maintainer, [@perronosaurio](https://github.com/perronosaurio),
privately. Reports are kept confidential.

Security problems are not conduct issues; report those as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What happens next

Depending on how serious it is, the maintainers may edit or hide the content, give a warning, lock the thread,
or block the person from the repository. Someone who harasses others doesn't get a second chance.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, shortened
for a project of this size.
